Where to launch besides Product Hunt

Product Hunt is busy, and a launch there can disappear in a day. Smaller launch sites often bring more honest feedback and a link that stays. We keep an updated list of free Product Hunt alternatives, and a where-to-launch finder that suggests places based on your product.

Free launch tools

  • Domain Rating checker: see how strong your site's links are.
  • Meta tag checker: preview how your page looks when shared.
  • AI crawler checker: check whether AI assistants can read your site.
  • Launch checklist: the steps before, during and after launch day.
  • Launch readiness grader: a quick score for your landing page.
